# Patuxent Electric: auditor's field notes

Visit on 2026-07-17. Shop and office in Prince Frederick. The owner, Raymond Dunmore, leads a crew on site most days
and pulls wire himself; the office administrator, Linda Marsh, runs the office and never goes to jobs. Some of the
electricians spent weeks on an out-of-town job in Hagerstown; they were paid a flat amount per day away on top of
wages, shown in its own column of the payroll register.
