# Annapolis Courier: business description

Application APP-AC-0529. Audit period July 7, 2025 to July 5, 2026.

Annapolis Courier runs same-day document and parcel delivery for law firms, title companies and clinics around
Annapolis, three van routes six days a week. Six employees, paid weekly. The owner, Gloria Rhee, has filed an
election of exclusion and is not on the payroll register.
