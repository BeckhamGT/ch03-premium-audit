# Harbor Point Legal: business description

Application APP-HPL-0102. Audit period July 7, 2025 to July 5, 2026.

Harbor Point Legal is a five-employee law office in Annapolis handling real estate closings, wills and small
business contracts. The founding partner, Catherine Harbor, has filed an election of exclusion and is not on the
payroll register. The firm has no other locations and uses no subcontractors.
