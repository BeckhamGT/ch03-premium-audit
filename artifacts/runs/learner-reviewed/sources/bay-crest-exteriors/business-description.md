# Bay Crest Exteriors: business description

Application APP-BCE-0217. Audit period July 7, 2025 to July 5, 2026.

Bay Crest Exteriors replaces roofs and installs siding and gutters on houses and small commercial buildings in Anne
Arundel and Queen Anne's counties. Ten employees. The owner, Philip Crestwood, has filed an election of exclusion and
is not on the payroll register. Bay Crest does its own installation and used no subcontractors in the period.
