# Classification guide excerpt

Linden Shore Mutual, workers' compensation, Maryland. Edition of July 2025. Internal use by underwriting and premium
audit.

This excerpt holds the general paragraphs and the class codes our current new-business employers fall under. Audit
files cite a paragraph by its identifier (CG-01, CG-02 and so on). A class code is cited by the paragraph that
describes it.

## General

**CG-01. Premium basis.** Premium is charged per USD 100 of payroll in each class code at the rate underwriting sets
for that code. Payroll is reported for the policy period being audited.

**CG-02. Assigning codes.** An employer is assigned the class code that describes its principal business. An employee
whose work is described by another code in this guide is reported under that code. An employer may carry several codes.

**CG-03. Payroll.** Payroll is the gross remuneration paid to employees for the period: wages, salaries, commissions
and bonuses. Expense reimbursements that the employer can document, such as travel and lodging costs, are not payroll.

**CG-05. Owners and executive officers.** An owner or executive officer who has filed an election of exclusion is not
counted. An owner who is included is counted under the code for the work the owner does.

**CG-06. Subcontractors.** Payments to a subcontractor who does not carry workers' compensation insurance for its own
workers may be treated as payroll of the employer, under the code for the work the subcontractor performed.

**CG-07. Division of payroll.** When an employee performs work in more than one classification and the employer keeps
records of the time spent in each, the employee's payroll may be divided between the codes.

## Class codes

**CG-04. C-110 Clerical office employees.** Employees whose duties are confined to an office: correspondence,
bookkeeping, dispatching by telephone or radio, drafting and estimating at a desk, and professional staff who work in
the office.

**CG-11. R-310 Roofing.** Installation, repair and removal of roofs and roof coverings on any building, including
work on scaffolds and ladders at the roof line.

**CG-12. R-320 Siding and gutters.** Installation and repair of exterior siding, gutters, downspouts and exterior trim
below the roof line. Roofing work is described by CG-11.

**CG-13. L-620 Landscaping and lawn care.** Lawn mowing, planting, mulching and the care of grounds.

**CG-15. H-420 Residential homebuilding.** Construction and remodeling of dwellings: framing, drywall, interior
finish, carpentry and general labor at the building site. Roofing work is described by CG-11 and electrical wiring
by CG-21.

**CG-21. E-510 Electrical wiring.** Installation and repair of electrical wiring and fixtures inside buildings,
including service connections.

**CG-22. M-830 Metal fabrication shop.** Cutting, bending and welding of sheet and structural metal in a shop.

**CG-31. T-610 Courier and delivery drivers.** Drivers of vans and light trucks delivering parcels and documents,
and helpers who ride on the delivery vehicles.

**CG-35. G-950 Parking garage attendants.** Parking and retrieving customer vehicles.

**CG-41. F-710 Restaurants.** Preparation and service of meals: kitchen staff, servers, hosts and dishwashers.

**CG-42. F-720 Off-site caterers.** Preparation of food and its service at events away from the employer's premises,
including the catering crew of an employer that also runs a restaurant.

**CG-51. N-910 Home health care.** Personal care and nursing services in clients' homes.
