# Larkspur Lane Bistro: business description

Application APP-LL-0611. Audit period July 7, 2025 to July 5, 2026.

Larkspur Lane Bistro is a 70-seat restaurant on Main Street in Annapolis, open for lunch and dinner. It also caters
weddings and office events at customers' venues with a small catering crew. Nine employees; the books are kept by an
outside accountant. The owner, Rosa Delacroix, has filed an election of exclusion and is not on the payroll register.
