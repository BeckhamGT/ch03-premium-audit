# Severn Homebuilders: auditor's field notes

Visit on 2026-07-16. Office in a converted house; three houses under construction. The office manager, Dana Whitcomb,
drives material and paperwork out to the sites and meets inspectors there; the owner calls it "running errands". The
estimator visits a site now and then to measure. The bookkeeper stays in the office.

Three subcontractors were paid in the period (framing, drywall and roofing). The owner says they are all insured; we
collected the certificates of insurance the owner could find.
