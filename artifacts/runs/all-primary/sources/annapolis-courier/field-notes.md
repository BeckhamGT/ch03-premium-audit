# Annapolis Courier: auditor's field notes

Visit on 2026-07-20. Dispatch office with two desks and a radio; three vans. While I was there, the dispatcher Lena
Ortiz left with the AR-2 van and came back at noon. The other dispatcher, Paul Haskins, was on the radio. The route log
lists every route day with its driver and anyone riding along. Pay weeks run Monday to Sunday.
