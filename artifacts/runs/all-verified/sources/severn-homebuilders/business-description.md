# Severn Homebuilders: business description

Application APP-SH-0417. Audit period July 7, 2025 to July 5, 2026.

Severn Homebuilders builds single-family houses in Anne Arundel County: framing, drywall, finish carpentry and site
labor with its own crew, and subcontractors for some trades. Eight employees. The owner, Martin Severn, has filed an
election of exclusion and is not on the payroll register.
