# Bay Crest Exteriors: auditor's field notes

Visit on 2026-07-15. Yard with two trucks, a material shed and a small office. The owner says most of the ten are
office and coordination staff. On the day of the visit eight people were out on two jobs, one roof and one siding job,
and the estimator was measuring a house for a quote. The bookkeeper works in the office.

Crew time is recorded on the crew timesheets by job and type of work. Office staff sign the site sign-in sheet when
they visit a job. The owner says siding is most of what the company sells now.
