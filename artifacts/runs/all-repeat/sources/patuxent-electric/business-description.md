# Patuxent Electric: business description

Application APP-PE-0388. Audit period July 7, 2025 to July 5, 2026.

Patuxent Electric does residential and light commercial wiring in Calvert and St. Mary's counties, plus a few
out-of-town jobs a year for a commercial client. Seven people on the payroll register, including the owner, Raymond
Dunmore, who has not filed an election of exclusion. No subcontractors in the period.
